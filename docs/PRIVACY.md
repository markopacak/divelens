# DiveLens privacy policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

DiveLens corrects underwater photos and videos on your phone. This policy explains what happens to your data. In short: **Photos and videos never leave your device.**

## What DiveLens processes

- **Photos and videos you pick.** DiveLens uses the Android photo picker, so it can only open the items you choose. It reads them, corrects their colours on your phone, and saves the corrected copies as new files in your gallery. The originals are not changed.
- **Metadata in your files.** Exported copies keep the date and time they were taken and their orientation. They do not keep the location (GPS) or other camera data. Your originals keep everything they had.
- **A few settings on your phone.** DiveLens remembers small things such as which tips you have already seen. They stay on your phone.

## What DiveLens does not do

- It has no accounts and no servers of its own.
- It does not upload, share or sell your photos, videos or any other data.
- It contains no advertising, analytics or crash reporting of its own.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** to show the progress of an export. You can refuse; exports still work.
- **Foreground service and keeping the phone awake:** so a long export keeps running while you use other apps.
- **Internet access and Google Play billing:** added by the Google Play Billing library, for purchases only. DiveLens sends no photos, videos or other data.

## Feedback by email

If you tap **Send feedback**, DiveLens opens your own email app with a draft that contains the app version, Android version and phone model. You can change or delete anything before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We receive only what you send, and use it only to answer you and improve the app. No photos or videos are attached.

## Purchases

If you buy the full version or donate, Google Play handles the payment. DiveLens does not see your payment details. To know whether the full version is active and how many exports are left in the free version, DiveLens stores only two things, on your phone: whether the full version is active, and today's export count. In the version from Google Play, purchases are handled by the Google Play Billing library. It communicates with Google Play and may send Google technical information about purchases. It never receives your photos or videos. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Children

DiveLens is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published in the app and at https://github.com/markopacak/divelens/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md with a new effective date.
